Straight Answers

Agent accountability,
straight answers

What is agent accountability?

The ability to attribute every autonomous agent action to a verified identity and a recorded decision — and to prove that record is complete and unaltered. It is the difference between "trust us" and "verify us."

Agent accountability vs agent security?

Security keeps bad agents out. Accountability proves what the good agents did. You need both: security stops compromise, accountability makes every authorized action auditable and attributable.

Do we really need this now?

EU AI Act Article 50 is live. SOC 2 buyers demand evidence. 88% of organizations report AI agent incidents — only 14% have full audit trails. The ones that get caught are the ones without receipts.

How does ProofChain differ from a logging tool?

Logs can be edited, deleted, or disputed. ProofChain anchors identity on-chain and writes tamper-evident, encrypted audit records to X1. A logging tool tells you what happened. ProofChain proves it.
