## Show HN Draft

**Title:** Show HN: ProofChain — Verifiable audit trails for AI agents (on-chain, EU AI Act ready)

**Body:**

AI agents act autonomously. Companies deploy them to make decisions, move money, and handle customer data. But when something goes wrong — and it will — there's no reliable record of what happened. Agent logs are mutable. Screenshots are worthless. Internal dashboards show what the agent says it did, not what actually happened.

ProofChain fixes this. Every agent action is cryptographically hashed and committed to the X1 blockchain as an HXMP record. Each agent gets a soulbound AgentID v2 NFT that anchors its identity on-chain. The audit trail is immutable — you can't edit it, you can't delete it, you can't pretend it didn't happen.

We built this in 48 hours. The demo has 100+ verifiable records on X1 mainnet right now.

**Why this matters now:**
- The EU AI Act requires traceability and auditability for high-risk AI systems. Article 12 mandates record-keeping. ProofChain gives you cryptographic evidence, not just a database entry.
- SOC 2 and GDPR compliance both require verifiable audit trails. Logs you can edit aren't audit trails — they're suggestions.
- The OpenAI agent breakout story showed what happens when nobody can verify what an agent did. Most failures won't be that dramatic. They'll be silent and boring — state drift, tool chain breaks, decisions that looked right to the agent but weren't.

**How it works:**
1. Register an AgentID v2 (soulbound NFT on X1)
2. Every agent action gets hashed and committed as an HXMP record
3. The dashboard shows a live, tamper-evident audit trail — not a log, a cryptographic proof

**Live demo:** https://proofchain.us — our agent Aster (AgentID v2, NFT 6LZaCMx2CUNH6aQ7kWcdaEBbucFrPrTKiKzkEvrtjqA3) is verifiable on-chain right now. The dashboard at https://proofchain.us/dashboard/ shows every action committed.

**GitHub:** https://github.com/ereezyy/proofchain

**Stack:** X1 blockchain, HXMP protocol, Solidity (AgentID v2 soulbound NFTs), on-chain hash verification

We built this because we think agent accountability shouldn't be optional. If your agents operate in regulated industries, you need cryptographic proof they did the right thing — not a log you trust them to maintain.
